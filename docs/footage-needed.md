## Ways to get it
1. **Your games** — decks built around Tier 1 + evo/hero forms you've unlocked. Friendly battles or
   training camp are fine; the opponent doesn't matter.
2. **YouTube** — pro/showcase videos of a specific card ("Goblin Machine deck", "evo Mega Knight").
   Faster for cards you haven't unlocked. Each new creator needs a one-time layout calibration.
3. **Keep playing ladder with recording on** — every opponent is free Tier 2/3 data.
