    The tiles you can use light up (the engine's own rule, every tick); the card's footprint, or a spell's radius, follows the cursor with its attack range (solid) and sight (dashed). A play lands on the next engine tick. Paused, a click queues the play and Z takes it back; it lands when you resume. Every game is recorded as before (data/live/situation_play.jsonl); a take-back is never recorded, because the play never reached the engine.
